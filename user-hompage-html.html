Book of the Day

The Maze Runner
James Dashner
About this book

When Thomas wakes up in the lift, the only thing he can remember is his name. He’s surrounded by stranger boys whose memories are also gone. Beyond the towering stone walls that surround them is a limitless, ever-changing maze. It’s the only way out—and no one’s ever made it through alive. Then a girl arrives. The first girl ever. And the message she delivers is terrifying: Remember. Survive. Run.
